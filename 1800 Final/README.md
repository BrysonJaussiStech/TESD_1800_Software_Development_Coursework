# 1800 Final

## Synopsis
I upgraded my calorie tracker! It works in three parts. The first part where you tell the program your target calorie level, the second part where you input your 
calories burned vs calories consumed, and the third part where you get a summary of your performance, based on the data on the second part.

## Motivation
I needed a simple calorie tracker. A lot calorie trackers have unneccesary ads and subscriptions, but mine is about as basic as you can get. 

## How to Run
You just need the .java file.
